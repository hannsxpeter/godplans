# Product business plan

Write a thorough implementation plan for a funded B2B scheduling product for
small physiotherapy clinics. A clinic is a workspace; the clinic owner invites
front-desk staff and therapists. Billing is per seat through Stripe, monthly or
annual, with a 14-day trial and a free tier limited to one therapist. An
enterprise tier for clinic chains is sold through a contact-sales form. Last
year's early-adopter plan is being retired, and its customers must keep their
price until renewal. The founder wants to know which number proves clinics get
value, and when to stop if they do not. There are no model calls.

Decide sensible defaults yourself rather than asking. Write your plan to
`PLAN.md`. Do not build the application.
