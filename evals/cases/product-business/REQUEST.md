# Product business evaluation

Use godplans to plan a funded B2B scheduling product for small physiotherapy
clinics. A clinic is a workspace; the clinic owner invites front-desk staff and
therapists. Billing is per seat through Stripe, monthly or annual, with a
14-day trial and a free tier limited to one therapist. An enterprise tier for
clinic chains is sold through a contact-sales form. Last year's early-adopter
plan is being retired, and its customers must keep their price until renewal.
The founder wants to know which number proves clinics get value, and when to
stop if they do not. There are no model calls.

Take recommended defaults. Produce the complete godplans artifact set under
`.godplans/`, including `PLAN.mdx` and its executable validator companion. Do
not build the application.
